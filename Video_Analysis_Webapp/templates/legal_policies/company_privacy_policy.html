This Privacy Policy describes how SHAIRE ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects the information of companies ("Client Companies" or "you") that visit our platform or engage our services.

2.2 Usage Data

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your company data. We may share information with:

  • Service Providers: Third-party vendors who assist in platform operations

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest

6. Data Retention

We retain company data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. Upon account termination, data is retained according to legal requirements and then securely deleted.

7. Your Rights

Client Companies have the right to:

  • Request deletion of data (subject to legal obligations)

  • Export data in a portable format

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance user experience, analyze usage, and improve our services. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your information in compliance with applicable data protection laws.

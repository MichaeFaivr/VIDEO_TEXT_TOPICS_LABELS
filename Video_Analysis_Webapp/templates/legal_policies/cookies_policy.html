1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit SHAIRE. They help us provide you with a better experience by remembering your preferences and understanding how you use our service.

2. How We Use Cookies

SHAIRE uses cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the platform to function properly
  • Performance Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our service
  • Functionality Cookies: Remember your preferences and settings
  • Analytics Cookies: Provide insights into usage patterns

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Cookie Type Purpose Duration
Session Cookies Maintain your login session Session only
Preference Cookies Remember your settings and preferences 1 year
Analytics Cookies Track usage and performance metrics 2 years
Security Cookies Protect against fraudulent activity Session only

4. Managing Cookies

You can control and manage cookies in several ways:

  • Most browsers allow you to refuse or accept cookies
  • You can delete cookies that are already stored on your device
  • You can set your browser to notify you when cookies are being sent

Note: Disabling cookies may affect the functionality of SHAIRE and limit your access to certain features.

5. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies may be set by third-party services that appear on our pages. SHAIRE does not control these cookies. Please refer to the third parties' websites for more information about their cookies.

6. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

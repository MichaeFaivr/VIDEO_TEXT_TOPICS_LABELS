🌟 Easy Contributions

EASY Verbatim Videos

Review a product you own, and show it, in a short video.

  • How you have been using the product
  • Show the product in use
  • What you like or dislike about it
  • Are you considering buying a new product of this type?
  • Do not include personal information
  • No special skills required - just a short video (2 to 8 minutes)

EASY VIDEO TYPE2

Add basic tags to help categorize content.

  • Select topic categories from predefined lists
  • Mark video language
  • Flag inappropriate content
  • Takes just a few clicks per video

EASY Quality Verification

Review existing transcriptions and labels for accuracy.

  • Confirm if transcriptions match audio
  • Vote on tag accuracy
  • Report errors or inconsistencies
  • Great for beginners!

🚀 Advanced Contributions

Detailed Topic Labeling

Provide in-depth categorization and analysis.

  • Identify key themes and subtopics
  • Add timestamps for topic changes
  • Create hierarchical topic structures
  • Requires domain knowledge

Sentiment Analysis

Evaluate emotional tone and speaker intent.

  • Mark positive/negative/neutral segments
  • Identify sarcasm or humor
  • Note emotional shifts

Entity Recognition

Identify and label important entities mentioned.

  • Tag people, places, organizations
  • Mark product names and brands
  • Link related entities
💡 Tip: Start with easy contributions to familiarize yourself with the platform. As you gain experience, you can move on to more advanced tasks that require specialized knowledge.

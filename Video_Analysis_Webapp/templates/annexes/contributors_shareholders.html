Contributors & Shareholders Program

Equity Sharing for First Contributors

Program Overview

We believe in rewarding our early contributors who help build and improve our platform. Our Contributors & Shareholders Program offers equity participation for validated contributions.

Key Benefits

  • Earn company shares through validated contributions
  • Quality and quantity of work determines share allocation
  • Limited to first 1 million contributors
  • Overall Maximum 10% of total Equity Pool for the Contributors
  • In Addition, Royalties may be granted based on contribution impact

Eligibility Rules

  1. Contribution Validation: All inputs must be validated by our quality assurance team to earn credits
  2. Share Allocation: Credits are converted to company shares up to a maximum of 10% total equity
  3. Contributor Limit: Limited to the first 1,000,000 contributors who make validated contributions
  4. Weighted Distribution: Share percentage is weighted based on both quantity and quality of contributions
  5. Maximum Individual Share: No single contributor can receive more than 5% of total company equity

How It Works

Step 1: Submit your contributions through the platform

Step 2: Our team validates the quality and accuracy of your work

Step 3: Validated contributions earn credits in your account

Step 4: Credits are converted to equity shares based on the weighted formula

Step 5: Receive official shareholding documentation

Service Availability

Initial Launch Regions: This webapp and the Contributors & Shareholders Program are initially available only in:

  • Europe
  • North America

Expansion to additional regions will be announced at a later date.

Important Notes

  • Program terms are subject to applicable securities laws and regulations
  • Shares are non-transferable during the initial contribution period
  • All equity allocations are subject to final legal documentation
  • Program may be modified or terminated at company discretion

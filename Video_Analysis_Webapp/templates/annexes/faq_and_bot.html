Credits & Billing

Q: How do I earn credits for video analysis?
A: You can earn credits by purchasing credit packages through our pricing page, referring new users to our platform (earn 10% of their first purchase), or by upgrading to a monthly subscription plan which includes bundled credits at discounted rates.
Q: What actions consume credits?
A: Credits are consumed based on video processing time and analysis complexity. Basic video analysis costs 1 credit per minute, advanced AI features (object detection, facial recognition) cost 2 credits per minute, and premium analysis with custom models costs 3 credits per minute.
Q: Do my credits expire?
A: Purchased credits are valid for 12 months from the date of purchase. Subscription plan credits reset monthly and do not roll over. Bonus credits earned through referrals expire after 6 months if unused.
